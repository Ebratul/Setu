## What it is \{#what-it-is}

Your buyer proposes a payment schedule: milestones such as **Production start**, **Shipment** and **Arrival**, each with an amount and the documents needed. Both sides must confirm it. Only then can the buyer put money in escrow.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="supplier" /> The supplier confirms its own side. The buyer confirms separately.

## When to use it \{#when}

When an order is **Proposed** and you accept the amounts, dates and required documents.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the order">
Go to **Orders** and click the order reference.
<Screenshot name="supplier/orders-list" route="/supplier" as="supplier" alt="Supplier orders list" caption="Pick the order." />
</Step>
<Step title="Read the milestones">
Under **Payment milestones** each card shows the milestone, the amount with its percent, the due date and the **Evidence** list of documents you must provide.
<Screenshot name="supplier/milestones" route="/supplier/SETU-2026-0001" as="supplier" alt="Payment milestones with amounts, due dates and the evidence checklist" caption="Check amounts, dates and evidence before you agree." wait="[data-testid='milestone']" />
</Step>
<Step title="Press Confirm schedule">
Click **Confirm schedule**. A box asks "Confirm the payment schedule?" and says you agree to the milestones, amounts and evidence shown. Confirm again. You see "Schedule confirmed."
<Screenshot name="supplier/confirm-dialog" route="/supplier/SETU-2026-0001" as="supplier" click="[data-testid='action-confirm']" alt="Dialog asking to confirm the payment schedule" caption="The confirmation dialog." />
</Step>
<Step title="Check the confirmation list">
A list shows Buyer and Supplier with **Confirmed** or **Not yet confirmed**. When both are confirmed, the order becomes **Confirmed**.
<Screenshot name="supplier/confirmations" route="/supplier/SETU-2026-0001" as="supplier" alt="List of party confirmations showing buyer and supplier status" caption="Both sides must be confirmed." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Proposed | Waiting for confirmation. |
| Locked | Schedule agreed. The step is waiting for funding and evidence. |
| Ready to release | Evidence is verified. The buyer can release the payment. |
| Waiting for approval | The buyer's release needs a human approval first. |
| Released | Paid to you (simulated). |
| Frozen | Paused for safety. |

Evidence badges: Missing, Pending, Verified, Flagged, Rejected.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- The button is greyed out when you already confirmed, or when there is no proposed schedule. The reason shows under the button.
- If the amounts are wrong, do not confirm. Ask your buyer to propose again. A new proposal resets confirmations.
- You cannot change amounts yourself.

## Related security notes \{#security}

<Callout type="security">
Confirming is recorded in the signed audit log. Money only moves after the buyer funds the escrow and the rules allow a release. See [permission tiers](/docs/en/security/permission-tiers).
</Callout>
