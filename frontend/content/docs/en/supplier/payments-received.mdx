## What it is \{#what-it-is}

Two blocks on the order page show your money: the green **Funds secured in escrow** box and the **Payments received** table. Everything is simulated. No real money moves.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="supplier" /> Read-only. Only your buyer can fund escrow and release payments.

## When to use it \{#when}

To check that your buyer has funded the order, and to see what was paid after each milestone.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Check the escrow box">
At the top of the order, the green box shows the amount held for you. The note says money is released milestone by milestone when the evidence is verified.
<Screenshot name="supplier/escrow-box" route="/supplier/SETU-2026-0001" as="supplier" alt="Green box saying funds secured in escrow with an amount" caption="Funds secured in escrow." />
</Step>
<Step title="Watch the milestones">
Each milestone shows a status. **Released** means paid. **Ready to release** means evidence is verified and the buyer can pay.
<Screenshot name="supplier/milestone-status" route="/supplier/SETU-2026-0001" as="supplier" alt="Milestone cards with status badges" caption="Milestone statuses." wait="[data-testid='milestone']" />
</Step>
<Step title="Read Payments received">
Each row is one paid milestone: **Milestone**, **Amount**, **FX payout** and **Posted**. FX payout shows the rate when the payment was converted to your currency, for example an amount at a rate.
<Screenshot name="supplier/payments-table" route="/supplier/SETU-2026-0001" as="supplier" alt="Payments received table with milestone, amount, FX payout and posted date" caption="Payments received." wait="[data-testid='payments']" />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Milestone | The step number that was paid. |
| Amount | Amount credited to you. |
| FX payout | The converted amount and the exchange rate used. A dash means no conversion. |
| Posted | Date of the payment. |
| No payments received yet | No milestone has been released. |

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- A milestone can be **Waiting for approval** or **Frozen**. The buyer's side is checking it. This is a safety step, not a rejection.
- Missing or flagged evidence delays release. Fix documents first.
- Payments go to your active payout account only. See [payout account](/docs/en/supplier/payout-account).

## Related security notes \{#security}

<Callout type="security">
Large or unusual releases need a human approval by the buyer (Tier 2 one-tap, Tier 3 with OTP and a 10 minute cooling-off). You are never asked for a code. See [permission tiers](/docs/en/security/permission-tiers).
</Callout>
