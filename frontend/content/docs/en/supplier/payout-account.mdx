## What it is \{#what-it-is}

The **Change payout account** form asks Setu to pay a new bank account for one order. Changing where money goes is a Tier 3 action, the highest level of care. Your buyer must approve it with a one-time code (OTP) and wait through a 10 minute cooling-off pause.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="supplier" /> <TierBadge tier={3} /> You send the request. The buyer approves it. You have no code to enter.

## When to use it \{#when}

Only when your company really changes bank. Use this form only. Never send new bank details by email, chat or inside a document.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the form">
On the order page, scroll to **Change payout account**. Choose the **Order** it applies to.

</Step>
<Step title="Fill in the account">
Enter **Bank name**, **Bank country (2 letters)**, **Account number**, **Account holder name**, **Account type** (Corporate or Personal) and **Currency** (USD, CNY or BDT). Use the exact company name on your licence.
<Screenshot name="supplier/payout-filled" route="/supplier/SETU-2026-0001" as="supplier" alt="Payout account form filled with sample bank details" caption="Check every field twice." />
</Step>
<Step title="Submit">
Press **Submit change request**. You see "Request sent to your buyer."
<Screenshot name="supplier/payout-sent" route="/supplier/SETU-2026-0001" as="supplier" alt="Success message after sending the change request" caption="The request is sent." />
</Step>
<Step title="Follow the status">
**Account change status** shows four steps and refreshes every 5 seconds: Request submitted, Supplier check, Buyer verification, New account active.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Waiting for buyer verification - Tier 3 | Buyer has not approved yet. |
| Approved: the new account is active | Future payments for this order go to the new account. |
| Rejected by the buyer | The old account stays. |
| Expired, Cancelled, Locked | The request ended. Nothing changed. Send a new one if needed. |

Until approval, **payments keep going to your current account**. The new account is never used before that.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- The change applies to one order only.
- Bank country is two letters, for example CN. The account number needs at least 6 characters.
- You may see a message that payment is paused while a change is pending.
- Setu's demo prefixes new account numbers with SIM-.

## Related security notes \{#security}

<Callout type="security" title="Why the buyer must approve">
Fraudsters often pretend to be a supplier and ask a buyer to pay a new account. So a payout change can never start from an email. It can only start here, inside your signed-in portal, and a person on the buyer side must approve it with OTP and a cooling-off pause. Requests in an email asking to pay another account are blocked and alerted. See [permission tiers](/docs/en/security/permission-tiers).
</Callout>
