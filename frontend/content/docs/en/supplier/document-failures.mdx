## What it is \{#what-it-is}

Every file you upload is checked by fixed rules. If the file does not pass, its badge says **Needs attention**. For safety, the screen does not show the reason. This page lists the usual causes and what to do.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="supplier" /> Anyone who uploads documents.

## When to use it \{#when}

When a document shows **Needs attention**, or a milestone stays on Pending, Flagged or Rejected.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Find the file">
On the order page, open **Documents**. Look for the badge **Needs attention**.

</Step>
<Step title="Compare with the order">
Open the order details and the milestone evidence list. Compare the file with the agreed order.
<Screenshot name="supplier/doc-compare" route="/supplier/SETU-2026-0001" as="supplier" alt="Order details block with quantity, unit price, incoterm and route" caption="Compare with the order details." />
</Step>
<Step title="Fix and upload again">
Correct the document at its source, export a fresh PDF and upload it. Ask your buyer if the problem is unclear.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

Common causes and the fix:

| Cause | What to do |
|---|---|
| Text that is invisible in the PDF (for example white, tiny or off-page text) | Re-export the PDF from your system without hidden text. |
| Sentences that give orders, such as "approve payment" | Remove them. Documents must only contain trade data. |
| Quantity, price, total or currency differ from the order | Correct the document or ask the buyer to update the order. |
| Company name, ports, dates, goods code or ship name differ from the order | Use exactly the same details everywhere. |
| Bank details differ from your registered account | Use your registered account, or follow the [payout account](/docs/en/supplier/payout-account) steps. |
| Packing list and bill of lading disagree (weight, packages) | Make them match. |
| The same file or invoice is already used on another deal | One document backs one deal. Upload the right one. |
| Unreadable, incomplete or hand-edited file | Upload a clean original. |
| A photo that does not look like the ordered goods | Take a clear photo of the actual goods. |

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- Do not edit PDFs by hand or copy a document from another order.
- Do not add notes to a document for the system or the buyer.
- Keep names, numbers and dates identical in all documents of one order.
- A file can also be marked for a buyer's review when something changed from the first proforma invoice.

## Related security notes \{#security}

<Callout type="security">
The checks protect you and your buyer from forged papers. Scores and details are hidden so they cannot be gamed. Text in a document is never treated as an instruction. See [permission tiers](/docs/en/security/permission-tiers).
</Callout>
