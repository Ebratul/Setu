## What it is \{#what-it-is}

The **Team** page lists the people who can sign in for your company. Owners can add or remove members.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="supplier" /> Owners manage the team. Finance and Viewer members can only look.

## When to use it \{#when}

When a colleague joins or leaves, or you want to check who has access.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Team">
Click **Team** (团队) at the top of the portal.
<Screenshot name="supplier/team-list" route="/supplier/team" as="supplier" alt="Team page with a table of members, roles and sign-in status" caption="Members table: Member, Role, Signed in once." />
</Step>
<Step title="Invite a member">
Under **Invite a member** enter **Email address**, **Name** and **Role**, then press **Add member**. No email is sent. Ask the person to register with this exact email address. The account links when they first sign in with it verified.
<Screenshot name="supplier/team-invite" route="/supplier/team" as="supplier" alt="Invite a member form with email, name and role" caption="Add a member." />
</Step>
<Step title="Remove a member">
Press **Remove** in the member's row and confirm "Remove this member?". They lose access to your company account.
<Screenshot name="supplier/team-remove" route="/supplier/team" as="supplier" alt="Remove this member confirmation dialog" caption="Confirm removal." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Manage the team and everything else. |
| Finance | Work with deals and payments. |
| Viewer | Only look, change nothing. |

"Signed in" means the person has signed in at least once. "Not signed in yet" means they have not registered yet.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- A company needs at least one owner. You cannot remove the last owner.
- An email that already belongs to a Setu account is refused.
- Your company must be approved before you can add members.
- Remove a person as soon as they leave your company.

## Related security notes \{#security}

<Callout type="security">
Only people on this list can act for your company. Keep the list short and up to date. See [permission tiers](/docs/en/security/permission-tiers).
</Callout>
