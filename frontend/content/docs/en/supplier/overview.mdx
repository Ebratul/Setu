## What it is \{#what-it-is}

The supplier portal is the part of Setu made for you, the seller. You see each order from your buyer, the money held for you in escrow, and what the buyer needs from you. Escrow means the buyer's money is held safely and released to you step by step. All money here is simulated sandbox data. No real payment is made.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="supplier" /> Supplier staff only. Other roles are sent to the buyer app. Page labels show English and Chinese side by side.

## When to use it \{#when}

- After your buyer opens an order with you.
- When you need to check the payment plan, send documents or see what you were paid.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Open your orders">
Sign in. The first page is **Orders** (订单). Each card shows the order reference, the buyer, **Order value**, **Secured in escrow** and the **Next milestone** with its due date.

</Step>
<Step title="Open one order">
Click the reference. The order page opens at the order route. The green box says **Funds secured in escrow** with the amount.

<Step title="Work down the page">
The order page has these blocks, top to bottom: escrow status, **Payment milestones**, **Documents**, **Payments received**, **Change payout account**, **Order details**. Use **All orders** to go back.

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Order value | Goods value of the order. |
| Secured in escrow | Money the buyer has already put in escrow for this order. |
| Next milestone | The next payment step and its due date. A dash means none is open. |
| Funds secured in escrow | Same amount, shown on the order page. |

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- If the list is empty ("No orders yet"), your buyer has not opened an order with you.
- Use the bell at the top for news about your payout account requests.
- The **Team** link at the top is for your company staff. See [Team](/docs/en/supplier/team).
- The page language can be switched at the top: English, Bangla or Chinese.

## Related security notes \{#security}

<Callout type="security">
You only see your own orders. You can send documents and confirm the plan. You cannot fund escrow, release payments or see any risk score. See [permission tiers](/docs/en/security/permission-tiers).
</Callout>
