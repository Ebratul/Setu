## What it is \{#what-it-is}

The deal page is the control room for one order. It has four tabs: **Overview**, **Documents**, **Analysis** and **Activity**. Overview holds the actions, the payment milestones, the escrow card and the ledger.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="buyer" /> Buyers (and admins) see all actions. Suppliers see a reduced view. Judges see it read-only.

## When to use it \{#when}

Use it to agree a payment schedule, fund the escrow, check evidence and release a payment. A **milestone** is one payment slice tied to a step of the order. A **tranche** means the same thing.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the deal">
Open **Deals**, then click the reference. The header shows the reference, status badge, **Simulated** badge, supplier and supplier-check badge. A red banner appears if the deal is disputed.

<Screenshot name="buyer/deal-header" route="/app/deals/SETU-2026-0001" as="buyer" alt="Deal page header with reference, status badge, Simulated badge, supplier name, supplier check badge and four tabs." caption="The deal header and tabs." />
</Step>
<Step title="Agree the schedule in Actions">
Under **Actions**, click **Propose milestones**. Choose 30% / 60% / 10% (production, shipment, arrival) or **Custom percentages** (2 to 5 tranches that add up to 100%). Then the supplier and you each click **Confirm schedule**. Proposing again clears confirmations. A disabled button shows the reason under it.

<Screenshot name="buyer/deal-actions" route="/app/deals/SETU-2026-0001" as="buyer" alt="Actions section with Propose milestones, Confirm schedule and Fund escrow buttons." caption="The Actions section." />
</Step>
<Step title="Follow the payment milestones">
Each milestone card shows its number, trigger (**Production start**, **Shipment** or **Arrival**), amount, percent, due date, a status badge and the required evidence. Evidence items are documents such as a proforma invoice, bill of lading or bill of entry.

<Screenshot name="buyer/deal-milestones" route="/app/deals/SETU-2026-0001" as="buyer" alt="Payment milestones list with amounts, status badges, due dates and evidence checklists." caption="Milestone cards with status and evidence." />
</Step>
<Step title="Release a payment">
When a milestone is **Ready to release**, click **Release payment**. A dialog shows the amount, supplier and receiving account. Confirm with **Release payment**. Setu then decides the tier. Tier 1 releases at once. Tier 2 or 3 waits in **Approvals**. See [Approvals](/docs/en/buyer/approvals).

<Screenshot name="buyer/deal-release" route="/app/deals/SETU-2026-0001" as="buyer" alt="Release milestone dialog showing the amount, supplier name and receiving account with a simulated note." caption="The release dialog." click="[data-testid='milestone'] button" />
{/* REVIEW: The click selector may hit a disabled Release button; the seeded deal's first milestone is waiting for approval, so the script may need another deal. */}
</Step>
<Step title="Check escrow, ledger and activity">
The **Escrow** card shows funded, released, refunded and in-escrow amounts. The ledger lists every posting. The **Activity** tab shows the signed trail of what happened.

<Screenshot name="buyer/deal-escrow-ledger" route="/app/deals/SETU-2026-0001" as="buyer" alt="Escrow card with a progress bar and amounts, next to deal details and the ledger table below." caption="Escrow card and ledger." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Milestone status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Proposed | Schedule suggested, not confirmed by both sides. |
| Locked | Confirmed, waiting for escrow money. |
| Ready to release | Covered by escrow and earlier tranches are settled. |
| Waiting for approval | A person must decide. No money moves yet. |
| Released | Paid out of escrow (simulated). |
| Frozen | Held, for example during a dispute or a Tier 3 request. |
| Refunded / Cancelled | Returned to you or never funded. |

| Evidence status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Missing / Pending | Document not yet uploaded or checked. |
| Verified | Checks passed. |
| Flagged / Rejected | Checks found a problem. |

The deal status runs Draft, Proposed, Confirmed, Funded, In production, Shipped, Arrived, Completed. It moves forward when milestones are released.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- **Release payment** is disabled until evidence is **Verified** and escrow covers the tranche. The reason is written under the button.
- Upload documents in the **Documents** tab. See [Documents](/docs/en/buyer/documents).
- **Analysis** links to the order analysis and to financing for this deal.

<Callout type="security">
Release is never done by the AI. The tier is calculated by code and rechecked at execution. Every step is in the [signed audit log](/docs/en/security/signed-audit-log).
