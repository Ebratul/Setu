## What it is \{#what-it-is}

The **Cash Flow** page forecasts your balance day by day for the next 90 days. It marks days when the balance falls below a minimum buffer, lists alerts, and has a **What-if planner**. The data is simulated.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="buyer" /> Buyers. Admins and judges first choose a business. Judges cannot change alerts.

## When to use it \{#when}

Before you fund or release a payment, and when the dashboard shows a cash shortfall alert.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Read the summary">
A banner says **First shortfall** with amount and date, or **No shortfall in the next 90 days**. Four cards show **Opening balance**, **Lowest balance**, **Total inflow** and **Total outflow**. The chart shows the running balance. **Show weekly table** gives week-by-week numbers.

<Screenshot name="buyer/cashflow-summary" route="/app/cashflow" as="buyer" alt="Cash flow page with a shortfall banner, four summary cards and a running balance chart." caption="Banner, summary cards and the 90-day chart." />
</Step>
<Step title="Use the calendar">
Each row is a week. Click a day to see **Details for** that date: opening and closing balance and each payment or receipt with its type and direction (In or Out). Days under the buffer are marked **Below the minimum buffer**.

<Screenshot name="buyer/cashflow-calendar" route="/app/cashflow" as="buyer" alt="Calendar of weeks and days with closing balances, with a details panel listing the payments of a selected day." caption="The calendar and the day details." />
</Step>
<Step title="Try the What-if planner">
You can **Move a payment** to a new date, **Add a loan inflow** (amount, arrival date, optional repayment), or tick items under **Switch items off**. A badge says **What-if - not saved**. Chart, calendar and **Scenario alerts** change. Click **Reset to saved forecast** to go back.

<Screenshot name="buyer/cashflow-whatif" route="/app/cashflow" as="buyer" alt="What-if planner with move a payment, add a loan and switch items off sections and a What-if not saved badge." caption="The What-if planner. Nothing is saved." />
</Step>
<Step title="Manage alerts">
Under **Alerts**, filter by **Type**, **Severity** and **Status**. Use **Acknowledge** (you saw it) or **Resolve** on an alert. Some alerts have an action link.

<Screenshot name="buyer/cashflow-alerts" route="/app/cashflow" as="buyer" alt="Alerts list with type, severity and status filters and Acknowledge and Resolve buttons." caption="Filtering and handling alerts." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Alert type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Cash shortfall | The balance drops below the buffer on a date. Critical if 7 days or less away, otherwise Warning. |
| Deadline | An unfunded milestone, customs duty, bill of entry or loan instalment is near. |
| FX opportunity | A cheaper day to convert is forecast. See [FX](/docs/en/buyer/fx). |
| Business check | A supplier check needs attention. |
| Fraud risk | A suspicious request was found. |
| Notice | Information only. |

Severity is **Critical**, **Warning** or **Info**. Status is **Open**, **Acknowledged** or **Resolved**. Alerts also resolve themselves when the condition goes away.

Inflows and outflows come from your recorded items, unfunded milestones and deal costs. The balance on a day is: opening balance plus inflows minus outflows.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- Nothing in the What-if planner changes real data. To act on a gap, go to [Financing](/docs/en/buyer/financing).
- The loan amount is prefilled with the amount that keeps you above the buffer.
- **Resolve** only closes the alert item. It does not remove a shortfall. Fix the cause.

## Related security notes \{#security}

<Callout type="security">
Forecasts and alerts are calculated by code from stored data. The AI assistant only explains them. A fraud-risk alert never executes anything on its own.
