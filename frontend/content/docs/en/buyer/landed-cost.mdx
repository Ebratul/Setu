## What it is \{#what-it-is}
The **Analysis** page (title: "Business financial analysis") has three tabs. **Landed Cost Calculator** is a what-if tool. **Monthly Summary** reviews one month of purchases. **Supplier Scorecard** compares your suppliers.

The selected tab is kept in the address (`?tab=calculator`, `monthly` or `suppliers`), so you can share a link to a tab.

## Who uses it \{#who}
<RoleBadge role="buyer" />

Admins and judges can open it too. They must first pick a business in the Monthly Summary tab.

## When to use it \{#when}
- To price a new order before you place it.
- At month end, to see purchases, landed cost and average margin.
- Before you choose a supplier for a repeat order.

## Step by step \{#how-to}
<Steps>
<Step title="Open Analysis">
Choose **Analysis** in the left menu. The calculator tab opens first.

<Screenshot name="buyer/landed-cost-1" route="/app/analysis" as="buyer" alt="Analysis page with three tabs and the what-if landed cost form" caption="The calculator starts with sample values." />
</Step>
<Step title="Fill the what-if form">
The form is called **What-if landed cost**. Set **Input currency** (USD, BDT or CNY), **Goods value**, **Quantity**, **Unit** and an optional **Selling price per unit**. Under **Extra cost lines** you can change each line, press **Remove**, or press **+ Add cost line**. Line types are Freight, Insurance, Duty & taxes, Bank charges and Other. You can have up to 20 lines.

Results update as you type. **Nothing is saved.**

<Screenshot name="buyer/landed-cost-2" route="/app/analysis" as="buyer" alt="What-if form with goods value, quantity, unit, selling price and extra cost lines" caption="Change any value and the result follows." />
</Step>
<Step title="Read the results">
You get the same cards, cost breakdown, target price helper and **Why these numbers?** panel as in [Order analysis](/docs/en/buyer/order-analysis).

<Screenshot name="buyer/landed-cost-3" route="/app/analysis" as="buyer" alt="Calculator results with landed cost, cost per unit, revenue and margin cards" caption="Results use today's mock exchange rates." />
</Step>
<Step title="Switch to Monthly Summary">
Pick a **Month**. You see **Orders**, **Total purchases**, **Total landed cost** and **Average margin**. **Key insights** gives plain sentences. **Last 6 months** is a chart with a data table. **Orders this month** links each order to its analysis.

<Screenshot name="buyer/landed-cost-4" route="/app/analysis?tab=monthly" as="buyer" alt="Monthly Summary tab with four cards, key insights and a six month chart" caption="One month at a glance." />
</Step>
<Step title="Switch to Supplier Scorecard">
The table has columns **Supplier**, **KYB**, **Score**, **On-time**, **Disputes**, **Price index** and **Your orders**. Click a column title to sort. The default sort is by score, lowest first, so weak suppliers come first.

<Screenshot name="buyer/landed-cost-5" route="/app/analysis?tab=suppliers" as="buyer" alt="Supplier scorecard table with weak suppliers highlighted" caption="Weak suppliers are flagged with a reason." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}
- **Price index** is 100 times landed cost divided by goods value. 100 means no extra costs. A higher number means more freight, duty and charges per unit of goods value.
- **Score** is the supplier's latest monthly score from 0 to 100. **On-time** and **Disputes** use the last months shown in the intro line.
- A supplier gets the **Weak supplier** badge, with reasons, if any of these is true: KYB status is not Verified, score below 70, dispute rate above 10%, or on-time rate below 80%.
- **Gross margin** bands are Healthy (15% or more), Thin (8% to 15%) and Low (below 8%).
- **Total purchases** can show a percentage change against the previous month.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}
- Use a dot for decimals. Numbers accept up to 4 decimals, for example `1250.50`. Quantity must be above 0.
- Unit is at most 8 characters.
- If a field has an error you see "Fix the highlighted fields to see results."
- Without a selling price, revenue and margin show a dash.
- The calculator uses mock exchange rates and the page says so.
