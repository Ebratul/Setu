## What it is \{#what-it-is}
**Ask Setu** is a chat assistant for your import business. It answers questions about cash flow, landed cost, financing, suppliers and documents. It also explains terms such as FOB.

Setu does the maths in code. The AI only reads the results and explains them in plain language. It gives options, not licensed financial advice. All data is simulated.

<RoleBadge role="buyer" />

## When to use it \{#when}
- You want a quick answer without opening several pages.
- You want a number explained in simple words.
- You want to know what the AI saw and what was hidden from it.

## Step by step \{#how-to}
<Steps>
<Step title="Open the chat">
Choose **Chat** in the left menu for the full page. On any other page, press <Kbd>/</Kbd> or the **Ask Setu** button at the bottom right for a side panel. Use **Open full chat** in the panel to continue on the full page.

<Screenshot name="buyer/chat-1" route="/app/chat" as="buyer" alt="Ask Setu chat page with the conversation list on the left and the message box at the bottom" caption="Full chat page with the Simulated badge." />
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a deal and ask">
Under **About deal:** pick a deal or keep **No specific deal**. Choose one of the **Try asking** questions or type your own, then press **Send**. <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> sends and <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> adds a new line. A message can be up to 4000 characters. Use **Stop** to cancel a reply.

<Screenshot name="buyer/chat-2" route="/app/chat" as="buyer" alt="Suggested questions such as Will I run short of cash this month" caption="Suggested questions help you start." />
</Step>
<Step title="Choose English or Bangla">
The reply language follows the language toggle at the top (**EN** or **বাংলা**). Switch it before you ask.

<Screenshot name="buyer/chat-3" route="/app/chat" as="buyer" alt="Top bar language toggle set to Bangla and a chat reply in Bangla" caption="The toggle sets the reply language." />
{/* REVIEW: the frontend sends language bn only when the UI is Bangla, else en (also for Chinese). The backend can detect script when no language is sent. */}
</Step>
<Step title="Read the cards">
Many answers include a card: **Landed cost**, **Cash-flow forecast**, **Alerts**, **Financing options**, **USD vs CNY payment**, **Summary for a month**, **Supplier scorecard**, **Supplier check**, **Document check** or **Registry**. Cards have buttons such as **Open analysis**, **Open cash flow** or **Open supplier profile**. A **Why:** line gives the reason in short.

<Screenshot name="buyer/chat-4" route="/app/chat" as="buyer" alt="Assistant answer with a landed cost card and an Open analysis button" caption="Cards show the calculated numbers." />
</Step>
<Step title="Check the sources">
When Setu answers from its knowledge base, it marks facts like [S1]. Press a marker to jump to the **Sources** list. Each source shows the title, the section, the reference, and "Last reviewed" date. A note says: "Simplified summaries - verify with the official source."

<Screenshot name="buyer/chat-5" route="/app/chat" as="buyer" alt="Answer with citation markers and a Sources list below it" caption="Press a marker to see the source." />
</Step>
<Step title="Open What did the AI see?">
Under a finished answer, press the eye button (**Show what the AI saw**). The window **What did the AI see?** has two sides. **What you see** shows your text. **What was sent to the AI** shows the same text with names, account numbers and similar details swapped for tokens.

<Screenshot name="buyer/chat-6" route="/app/chat" as="buyer" click="[data-testid=xray-button]" alt="What did the AI see window comparing the original text with the masked text" caption="Highlighted values are replaced before the AI sees them." />
</Step>
<Step title="Manage conversations">
The left list has **New chat**, **Search chats**, rename and delete. Delete asks you to confirm. Use the thumbs up or down to rate an answer. **Copy** copies the text.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}
- **Privacy counter**: "Identifiers protected this session: N" and "Leaked: N". Leaked should stay 0.
- **Security check** chip under an answer: lists what was checked (documents, knowledge base, party checks, registry). It also says when Setu ignored an instruction found in a document, email or tool result, or when a deal was flagged for extra verification (Tier 3).
- **Simulated data** badge: the answer includes simulated figures.
- "Some passages were withheld because they contained instruction-like text": a source was removed because it tried to give orders.
- Stop messages: "This conversation reached its size limit", "Setu stopped after using too many tools for one question", "The assistant could not finish this answer".
- Steps like "Analysing the order costs..." show which tools Setu is using while it works.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}
- Select a deal first when you ask about one order. On a deal or financing page the drawer picks it for you.
- Ask one thing at a time.
- Setu only talks about your import business. Other topics get a polite refusal.
- A "pay" or "release" request does not move money. At most Setu sends a request to **Approvals**.

## Related security notes \{#security}
<Callout type="security" title="The AI can only read and propose">
The assistant can use Tier 0 and Tier 1 tools only. For Tier 2 and Tier 3 actions it can only create a pending request. A person must approve it. See [Permission tiers](/docs/en/security/permission-tiers).
</Callout>
<Callout type="security" title="Masked before it leaves Setu">
Personal and account details are replaced by tokens before any model call, and put back afterwards by code. See [Privacy gateway](/docs/en/security/privacy-masking).
</Callout>
