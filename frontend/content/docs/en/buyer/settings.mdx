## What it is \{#what-it-is}
Setu has no single "Settings" page. Display options sit in the top bar of every page. Team management is on the **Team** page.

## Who uses it \{#who}
<RoleBadge role="buyer" />

Only **Owners** can add or remove team members. Other members see the list only.

## When to use it \{#when}
- You prefer Bangla or a dark screen.
- A new colleague must sign in for your company.
- Someone leaves your company.

## Step by step \{#how-to}
<Steps>
<Step title="Find the display controls">
The group "Display and account settings" in the top bar has **Search**, the language toggle, the number style button, the theme button, the notification bell and your name with **Switch user** and **Log out**.

<Screenshot name="buyer/settings-1" route="/app" as="buyer" alt="Top bar with search, language toggle, theme button, notifications and the user menu" caption="Display controls live in the top bar." />
</Step>
<Step title="Change the language">
Choose **EN** or **বাংলা** in the **Language** toggle. The whole app, the chat replies and the explanations switch. Setu remembers your choice in this browser.

<Screenshot name="buyer/settings-2" route="/app" as="buyer" click="[role=radio]:has-text('বাংলা')" alt="The app in Bangla after choosing the language toggle" caption="The app after switching to Bangla." />
{/* REVIEW: in the buyer app the toggle shows only EN and Bangla. The Chinese option (中文) is shown in the supplier portal, /verify and public pages (withZh), not in the buyer top bar. */}
</Step>
<Step title="Pick Bangla or Latin digits">
While Bangla is on, a number-style button appears. It switches between Bangla digits (১২৩) and Latin digits (123). Bangla digits are the default. The button is hidden in English.

<Screenshot name="buyer/settings-3" route="/app" as="buyer" alt="Number style button showing Bangla digits" caption="Switch between ১২৩ and 123." />
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a theme">
The theme button cycles **System**, **Light**, **Dark**. Its label names the current mode.

<Screenshot name="buyer/settings-4" route="/app" as="buyer" click="[data-testid=theme-toggle]" alt="The app after the theme button was pressed" caption="The theme button cycles through three modes." />
</Step>
<Step title="Open the Team page">
Choose **Team** in the left menu. **Members** lists each person with **Role** and **Signed in once** (Signed in, or Not signed in yet).

<Screenshot name="buyer/settings-5" route="/app/team" as="buyer" alt="Team page with a members table showing role and sign-in status" caption="Your company's members." />
</Step>
<Step title="Add a member">
Under **Invite a member**, enter **Email address**, **Name** and **Role**, then press **Add member**. Setu sends **no email**. Ask the person to register with that exact email. Their account links the first time they sign in with it verified.

<Screenshot name="buyer/settings-6" route="/app/team" as="buyer" alt="Invite a member form with email, name and role fields" caption="No email is sent. Tell the person yourself." />
</Step>
<Step title="Remove a member">
Press **Remove** on a row and confirm **Remove this member?**. They lose access to your company's account.

<Screenshot name="buyer/settings-7" route="/app/team" as="buyer" alt="Confirmation dialog asking to remove a member" caption="Removing asks you to confirm." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}
| Role | What the screen says |
|---|---|
| Owner | Can manage the team and everything else. |
| Finance | Can work with deals and payments. |
| Viewer | Can only look. Cannot change anything. |

Errors you may see:
- "This email already belongs to a Setu account."
- "Your company must be approved before you can add members."
- "A company needs at least one owner. Make someone else an owner first."
- "This account is not linked to a company, so it has no team."

The backend refuses changes from a Viewer ("viewers can read but not change anything").

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}
- Give new people the Viewer role first if they only need to look.
- Use the exact email you added. A different address will not link.
- Keep at least two Owners so you are never locked out.
- Keyboard: <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>K</Kbd> opens search, <Kbd>/</Kbd> opens the chat, <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> closes a dialog.
- The "Simulated" badge in the top bar stays on every page. Money in Setu is not real.
