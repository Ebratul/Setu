## What it is \{#what-it-is}

The Dashboard is the first screen after you sign in. It shows the state of your business in one view. All money on it is simulated sandbox data, and the **Simulated** badge sits next to the title.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="buyer" /> Buyers (importers) use it every day. Admins and judges can open it too. They first pick a business in a **Select a business** list.

## When to use it \{#when}

- At the start of the day, to see what needs attention.
- After a payment or an approval, to check that cash and escrow changed.
- To jump to a deal, the approvals inbox or the cash-flow calendar.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Read the four summary cards">
The top row shows **Cash balance**, **In escrow**, **Due in next 30 days** and **Open alerts**. Use the currency switch (USD, BDT, CNY) to change the currency of the money cards. The numbers are calculated by the server. The screen only displays them.

<Screenshot name="buyer/dashboard-kpis" route="/app" as="buyer" alt="Dashboard top row with four cards: cash balance, in escrow, due in next 30 days and open alerts, plus a USD, BDT, CNY currency switch." caption="The four summary cards and the currency switch." />
</Step>
<Step title="Check alerts and pending approvals">
**Alerts** lists open alerts with a severity and a type. **Manage alerts** opens the cash-flow page. The **Pending approvals** card shows a count. **Open approvals inbox** takes you to the approvals page.

<Screenshot name="buyer/dashboard-alerts" route="/app" as="buyer" alt="Dashboard showing the alerts panel on the left and the pending approvals count on the right." caption="Alerts and the pending approvals count." />
</Step>
<Step title="Look at this month and the 90-day cash chart">
**This month** shows the number of orders, total purchases, total landed cost and average margin. **Open monthly summary** goes to Analysis. **Cash flow, next 90 days** is a small chart. It shows a green tick when no shortfall is expected. **Open cash flow** opens the full page.

<Screenshot name="buyer/dashboard-month-chart" route="/app" as="buyer" alt="Dashboard cards for this month and the 90 day cash flow mini chart." caption="Monthly figures and the 90-day cash chart." />
</Step>
<Step title="Open an active deal">
The **Active deals** table lists reference, supplier, status, goods value and the **Next milestone** with amount and due date. Click a reference to open the deal.

<Screenshot name="buyer/dashboard-active-deals" route="/app" as="buyer" alt="Active deals table with reference, supplier, status badge, goods value and next milestone columns." caption="Active deals and their next milestone." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Cash balance | Your simulated cash on hand. |
| In escrow | Money you have deposited that is still held for suppliers. |
| Due in next 30 days | Payments the forecast expects in the next 30 days. |
| Open alerts | Alerts not yet resolved. See [Cash flow](/docs/en/buyer/cash-flow). |
| Pending approvals | Requests waiting for a person to decide. See [Approvals](/docs/en/buyer/approvals). |
| Next milestone | The next payment slice of the deal, with its due date. A dash means none is left. |

Landed cost is the full cost of getting goods to your warehouse: price, freight, insurance, duty and bank charges.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- The checklist card at the top is only a first-visit guide. You can dismiss it and show it again.
- If a number looks wrong, check the currency switch first.
- A zero in **Pending approvals** is normal. It means nothing waits for you.
