## What it is \{#what-it-is}
You can pay a Chinese supplier through a USD route or directly in CNY. **FX** means foreign exchange. Each route has bank spreads and fees, so the total cost in BDT differs. Setu compares both routes and shows the cheaper one.

There is no separate FX page. The comparison appears as a card in the chat assistant. A **rate lock** holds today's rate for 24 hours. You ask for it in chat, and you approve it on the Approvals page.

All rates, spreads and fees are mock demo values. No real money moves.

## What the results mean \{#results}
- **Sent**: the amount sent in the route currency before fees.
- **Rate used**: the BDT per unit of that currency, including the bank spread.
- **Conversion**: BDT cost of the conversion.
- **Fees**: SWIFT and correspondent fees on the USD route, a flat transfer fee on the CNY route.
- **Total**: Conversion plus Fees in BDT.
- The supplier must receive the CNY value of the invoice. On the USD route, you send enough USD to cover the supplier bank's conversion loss.

Mock assumptions used today in the code:

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Bank sells USD | mid rate + 0.50% |
| Supplier bank converts USD to CNY | mid rate - 0.60% |
| USD route fees | SWIFT USD 20 + correspondent USD 15 |
| Bank sells CNY | mid rate + 0.80% |
| CNY route fee | BDT 1,000 |

A lock holds the rate for 24 hours for the amount you asked. If no amount is given, it covers the unreleased milestones.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}
- A lock only makes sense when the supplier account is in a different currency from the deal.
- While a lock is active, a conversion release does not need the extra Tier 2 approval for "rate not locked".
- The saving can be small. Check the total, not just the rate.
- A second lock on the same deal cancels the first.

## Related security notes \{#security}
<Callout type="security" title="Locking a rate is Tier 2">
The AI can only ask. A person approves with one tap. The request is bound to the deal, the amount and the currencies, and it is written to the signed audit log. See [Permission tiers](/docs/en/security/permission-tiers).
</Callout>
