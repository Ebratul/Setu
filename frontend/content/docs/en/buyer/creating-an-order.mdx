## What it is \{#what-it-is}

A **deal** is one import order with one supplier. The **Deals** page lists your deals. **New deal** opens a four-step form that creates a **draft** deal. Creating a draft moves no money.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="buyer" /> Buyers create deals. Admins can too. Suppliers and judges do not see the **New deal** button.

## When to use it \{#when}

When you agree to buy goods and want to track cost, milestones and escrow in one place.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Deals and find your deal">
Open **Deals** in the menu. Filter by status (All, Draft, Proposed, Confirmed, Funded, In production, Shipped, Arrived, Completed, Disputed, Cancelled) or search by reference, goods or supplier. Click **New deal**. If you have no deals yet, the button is called **Create a deal**.

<Screenshot name="buyer/deals-list" route="/app/deals" as="buyer" alt="Deals page with status filter chips, a search box, a New deal button and a table of deals." caption="The Deals list with filters and the New deal button." />
</Step>
<Step title="Step 1 - Supplier">
Choose one supplier. Each has a supplier-check badge (KYB, which means Know Your Business). If the badge is Warning or Blocked, a dialog asks you to confirm with **Use this supplier anyway**. Paying a blocked supplier is strongly discouraged and the system may refuse it later.

<Screenshot name="buyer/deals-new-supplier" route="/app/deals/new" as="buyer" alt="New deal form step 1 showing a list of suppliers with radio buttons and KYB badges." caption="Step 1: choose a supplier." />
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2 - Goods">
Fill in **Goods**, **HS code** (4 to 12 digits and dots, for example 6109.10), **Quantity**, **Unit**, **Unit price** and **Input currency** (USD or CNY). **Expected sale value** is optional. The **Goods total** is a preview. The system calculates the final value.

<Screenshot name="buyer/deals-new-goods" route="/app/deals/new" as="buyer" alt="New deal form step 2 with goods, HS code, quantity, unit, unit price and currency fields and a goods total preview." caption="Step 2: describe the goods." />
{/* REVIEW: Steps 2 to 4 need the form to be filled before they appear. The screenshot script has to type into the form or the image will show step 1. */}
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3 - Shipping and costs">
Pick the **Incoterm** (for example FOB), the **Port of loading** and **Port of discharge**. Add optional extra costs: freight, insurance, duty and taxes, bank charges. A **Landed cost preview** appears with the total and the cost per unit.

<Screenshot name="buyer/deals-new-shipping" route="/app/deals/new" as="buyer" alt="New deal form step 3 with incoterm, ports, extra cost fields and a landed cost preview card." caption="Step 3: shipping, extra costs and the landed cost preview." />
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4 - Review and create">
Check the summary. Click **Create draft deal**. You land on the new deal page with a reference such as SETU-2026-0001.

<Screenshot name="buyer/deals-new-review" route="/app/deals/new" as="buyer" alt="New deal review step listing supplier, goods, quantity, total, incoterm and route with a Create draft deal button." caption="Step 4: review and create the draft." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | The deal exists but has no payment schedule yet. |
| Landed cost preview | Total cost to land the goods, and per unit. Calculated by the system, simulated. |
| Supplier badge | Result of the supplier check. See [Suppliers and KYB](/docs/en/buyer/suppliers-kyb). |

After creation you propose milestones, both sides confirm, then you fund the escrow. See [Deal detail](/docs/en/buyer/deal-detail).

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- Use a period for decimals. Quantity and unit price must be above zero.
- The landed cost preview can be unavailable. You can still create the deal.
- If creation fails, the red message at the bottom of the review step says why. Nothing is saved.

## Related security notes \{#security}

<Callout type="security">
A supplier check runs when the deal is created. A blocked supplier cannot be paid. Details: [Permission tiers](/docs/en/security/permission-tiers).
</Callout>
