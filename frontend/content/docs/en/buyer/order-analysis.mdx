## What it is \{#what-it-is}
Order Analysis shows what one order really costs to land in Bangladesh. It adds freight, insurance, duty and bank charges to the goods value. It then shows the cost per unit, your margin and the price at which you break even.

**Landed cost** means the full cost of goods once they reach your door, not just the invoice value.

The numbers come from Setu's calculation code, not from the AI. The chat assistant only explains them. All data is simulated.

## Who uses it \{#who}
<RoleBadge role="buyer" />

Buyers see their own orders. Admins and judges can read any order.

## When to use it \{#when}
- Before you agree a price with your customers.
- When an order's costs change and you want the new margin.
- When you want a quick answer to "Am I still making money on this order?".

## What the results mean \{#results}
| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Landed cost | Goods value plus all extra cost lines. |
| Cost per unit | Landed cost divided by quantity. |
| Expected revenue | Quantity times your selling price. Shows a dash if no price is set. |
| Gross margin | Profit divided by revenue. It comes with a band badge. |
| Break-even selling price per unit | The price at which profit is zero. |

Margin bands use the margin rounded to two decimals:

| Band | Margin |
|---|---|
| Healthy | 15% or more |
| Thin | 8% up to 15% |
| Low | Below 8% |

The line "Simulated data. Exchange rates as of ..." lists the mock rates used for conversion.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}
- A dash in **Expected revenue** or **Gross margin** means no selling price is set for the order.
- The target margin must be a number below 100 with at most 4 decimals.
- A band is never shown by colour alone. It always has an icon and a word.
- To test other numbers without changing the order, use the [Landed Cost Calculator](/docs/en/buyer/landed-cost).

## Related security notes \{#security}
<Callout type="security" title="Code calculates, AI explains">
Costs, margins and bands are computed by deterministic code with exact decimal money. The AI never produces a number. See [Permission tiers](/docs/en/security/permission-tiers).
