## What it is \{#what-it-is}

Before a buyer can create real orders or move simulated money, a reviewer verifies the company. This page lists exactly what the **Business** and **Documents** steps ask for.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="buyer" /> Bangladeshi importers setting up their own account.

## When to use it \{#when}

Keep this list at hand before you start `/onboarding`, or when a reviewer asks for a correction.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Have your documents ready">
You need a **Trade licence** and an **Import Registration Certificate (IRC)**. A **TIN certificate** and other supporting files are optional. Each file is a PDF, PNG or JPG, up to 4 MB.
<Screenshot name="buyer/verification-documents" route="/onboarding?step=documents" as="buyer" alt="The Documents step listing Trade licence and IRC as required." caption="Required and optional documents." />
</Step>
<Step title="Fill in the company fields">
Enter Legal company name, Trade name (optional), Trade licence number, Issuing authority, Licence expiry date, IRC number and TIN.
<Screenshot name="buyer/verification-company" route="/onboarding?step=business" as="buyer" alt="The Business step company group with licence, IRC and TIN fields." caption="Company details." />
</Step>
<Step title="Add address and import profile">
Enter Division, District, Upazila and Street address, then Business type (Manufacturer or Trader), Main goods you import and Monthly import volume (USD range).
<Screenshot name="buyer/verification-address" route="/onboarding?step=business" as="buyer" alt="The Business step address and import profile groups." caption="Address and import profile." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Field | Format Setu checks |
|---|---|
| Mobile number | `+8801` followed by 3 to 9 and eight digits. Local `01712345678` is accepted and converted. |
| Trade licence number | Letters, digits, slash, hyphen or dot, as printed on the licence. |
| IRC number | Letters, digits, slash or hyphen. |
| TIN | 9 to 13 digits. |
| Licence expiry date | A future date in YYYY-MM-DD. |

The server applies the same rules, so a field marked wrong in red must be corrected before you can continue. Some formats can be tuned by the deployment, so a number that looks right may still be refused: check it against the licence.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- Use the legal name exactly as on the trade licence.
- An expired licence is refused. Renew it first.
- If the same licence is already registered, your application is not rejected automatically. It goes to a manual review.
- Uploading the same file under another account is flagged.
