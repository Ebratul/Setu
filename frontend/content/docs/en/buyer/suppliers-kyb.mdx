## What it is \{#what-it-is}
**KYB** means "know your business". Setu checks a supplier's licence, its bank account and its deal history, and shows the result as a badge with a score from 0 to 100.

The check is deterministic code. There is no AI in it. Sanctions screening uses a fictional demo list. All data is simulated.

## Who uses it \{#who}
<RoleBadge role="buyer" />

Buyers and admins can run **Re-run KYB**. Judges have a read-only view.

## When to use it \{#when}
- Before the first deal with a new supplier.
- When a supplier sends new bank details.
- When a deal shows a supplier warning.

## What the results mean \{#results}
| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified | All checks passed. |
| Warning | At least one check warned or failed. Look at the reasons. |
| Blocked | Sanctions screening failed. Do not send any payment. |
| Pending | No result yet. |

{/* REVIEW: the exact backend situation that produces the Pending badge was not traced; wording is a guess. */}

Checks and what they test:

| Check | Tests |
|---|---|
| Licence name vs invoice seller | Name on the licence matches the invoice. |
| Licence name vs account holder | The account belongs to the licensed company. |
| Account type | A personal account for a company fails. |
| Account age | Under 7 days fails, under 30 days warns. |
| Bank country | A bank in another country warns. |
| Pending account change | A new account always warns. |
| Deal history | Many disputed deals warn. |
| Sanctions screening | Any name hit blocks the supplier. |
| Receiving account | No active account on file warns. |

The **Check score** starts at 100 and loses points per warning or failure. A sanctions failure sets it to 0.

Names are matched after cleaning up case, punctuation and company suffixes. A match score of 92 or more passes. 80 to 91 warns. Below 80 fails.

The supplier badge comes from the active receiving account. A pending account is shown apart.

On the **Analysis** page, a supplier is also marked **Weak supplier** by score, disputes and on-time rate. See [Landed cost and scorecard](/docs/en/buyer/landed-cost).

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}
- A Warning is not a block. It asks you to look at the reason first.
- A Blocked badge means "do not pay". The deal page shows "This supplier is blocked. Do not send any payment for this deal."
- "Live evaluation, not stored yet" means the result is shown but not saved.
- Never trust an email that asks you to change the bank account. Use [Check an email](/docs/en/buyer/email-check).

## Related security notes \{#security}
<Callout type="security" title="Changing a bank account is Tier 3">
Moving money to a new account needs OTP and a 10 minute cooling-off. KYB informs this decision but does not replace it. See [Permission tiers](/docs/en/security/permission-tiers).
</Callout>
