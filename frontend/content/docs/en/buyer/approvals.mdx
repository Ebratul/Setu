## What it is \{#what-it-is}

The **Approvals** page is your inbox for money and account changes that need a person to decide. Setu never decides these for you. The AI assistant can only queue a request. A human click executes it.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="buyer" /> The buyer of the deal, or an admin. Suppliers, judges and other buyers cannot decide.

## When to use it \{#when}

When the dashboard shows pending approvals, or after you clicked **Release payment** and the milestone says **Waiting for approval**.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the inbox">
Open **Approvals**. Two tabs: **Pending** and **History**. Each card shows the action, a <TierBadge tier={2} /> or <TierBadge tier={3} /> badge, a status, deal, supplier, amount, who asked, and **Why Setu asked you** reasons. **Evidence snapshot** expands the facts used (supplier check, documents, FX rate, rule trace).

<Screenshot name="buyer/approvals-list" route="/app/approvals" as="buyer" alt="Approvals page with Pending and History tabs and approval cards showing tier badges, amounts, reasons and a Review button." caption="The approvals inbox." />
</Step>
<Step title="Tier 2 - one tap">
Click **Review**. The dialog is titled Approve with the action name. It explains **What will happen**. Click **Approve** to confirm or **Reject** to refuse. Setu checks the facts again before anything moves.

<Screenshot name="buyer/approvals-tier2" route="/app/approvals" as="buyer" alt="Tier 2 approval dialog with a summary, what will happen box, reasons, and Reject and Approve buttons." caption="A Tier 2 approval dialog." click="[data-testid='review-btn']" />
</Step>
<Step title="Tier 3 - code and cooling-off">
Tier 3 cards show a **Cooling-off** timer. In the dialog, a ring counts down the pause. Setu sends a 6-digit code to your phone (a simulated SMS). In this sandbox, open **Demo phone**, **Copy code** and paste it into **6-digit code**. **Approve** unlocks only when the pause is over and all 6 digits are entered. **Resend code** and **Cancel request** are available.

A toast says **Approved and carried out (simulated)** or **Request rejected. Nothing was paid or changed.** The card moves to **History**. Done requests have a receipt button that opens the signed audit entry.

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Tier | What it needs | Typical triggers |
|---|---|---|
| <TierBadge tier={0} /> | Nothing. Runs by itself. | Read-only analysis and checks. |
| <TierBadge tier={1} /> | Nothing. You get a notice. | Release to a verified, unchanged account, USD 20,000 or less, no warnings. |
| <TierBadge tier={2} /> | One tap on **Approve**. | First deal with a supplier, FX lock, document warning, anything proposed by the AI. |
| <TierBadge tier={3} /> | 6-digit code and a 10-minute cooling-off. | Over USD 20,000, new or changed beneficiary (receiving account), high fraud score, duplicate document, an instruction found inside a document or email. |

Some facts block the action altogether, such as a sanctions hit or a blocked supplier.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | Waiting for you. |
| Approved | You said yes, but the action could not run yet (for example evidence missing). Approve again, no new code. |
| Done | Carried out (simulated). |
| Rejected / Cancelled / Expired | Nothing was paid or changed. |
| Locked | Three wrong codes. Reject it and ask for a new request. |

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- Codes live 5 minutes. A wrong code costs one of 3 tries. After the third, the request locks.
- A pending Tier 3 request freezes the escrow of your deals until you decide.
- Requests expire after 24 hours.
- A request that came by email shows a red warning. Setu never changes an account from an email. Reject it.
- If conditions get worse before you approve, Setu cancels the request and opens a stricter one (**Setu needs a stricter approval**).
