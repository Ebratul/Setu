## What it is \{#what-it-is}

**Escrow** is a holding account. You deposit money, and it stays there until a milestone is released to the supplier. In Setu this is a simulated sandbox. No real funds move.

This action lives on the deal page: **Deals**, open a deal, **Overview** tab, **Actions** section, button **Fund escrow**. It has no page of its own.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="buyer" /> Buyers and admins. Suppliers and judges do not see the button.

## When to use it \{#when}

After you and the supplier both clicked **Confirm schedule** (deal status Confirmed). Fund before you want a milestone released.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Check that funding is allowed">
On the deal page, look at **Actions**. **Fund escrow** is enabled only when the server allows it. If it is grey, read the reason below it. The usual reason is that the schedule is not confirmed yet.

<Screenshot name="buyer/escrow-fund-button" route="/app/deals/SETU-2026-0001" as="buyer" alt="Actions section with the Fund escrow button next to the escrow card showing the still to deposit amount." caption="The Fund escrow button." />
</Step>
<Step title="Open the dialog and choose an amount">
Click **Fund escrow**. The dialog says how much is **Still to deposit**. Leave **Amount** empty to deposit the next tranche. Or type an amount with at most 2 decimals. You can deposit tranche by tranche or the whole goods value.

<Screenshot name="buyer/escrow-fund-dialog" route="/app/deals/SETU-2026-0001" as="buyer" alt="Fund the escrow dialog with the still to deposit text, an optional amount field and a simulated note." caption="The Fund the escrow dialog." click="[data-testid='action-fund']" />
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm and read the result">
Click **Fund escrow** in the dialog. A message says **Escrow funded (simulated).** Your cash balance goes down. The escrow card shows the new **Funded** and **In escrow** amounts. A milestone becomes **Ready to release** once deposits cover it and earlier tranches are settled.

<Screenshot name="buyer/escrow-after-fund" route="/app/deals/SETU-2026-0001" as="buyer" alt="Escrow card after funding with funded, released, refunded and in escrow amounts and a coloured progress bar." caption="The escrow card after a deposit." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Escrow card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Funded | Total you have deposited. |
| Released | Total already paid to the supplier. |
| Refunded | Total returned to you. |
| In escrow | Funded minus released and refunded. |
| Still to deposit | Goods value not yet deposited. |
| Settles to the supplier in CNY | The supplier is paid in another currency. The FX rate is shown after a release. See [FX](/docs/en/buyer/fx). |

A warning line appears when deposits go above $20,000: **Deposits above $20,000 need extra approval before any release.**

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- A deposit larger than your cash balance is refused.
- Deposits above the goods value not yet deposited are refused.
- Funding does not pay the supplier. Release does. See [Deal detail](/docs/en/buyer/deal-detail).

## Related security notes \{#security}

<Callout type="security">
The $20,000 limit is strict: exactly $20,000.00 is Tier 1, $20,000.01 is Tier 3. If an AI assistant suggests funding, it can only queue a request for you. Only your click executes it. See [Permission tiers](/docs/en/security/permission-tiers).
</Callout>
