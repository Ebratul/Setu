## Measured evaluation (NFR-4) \{#eval}

From the latest evaluation run on the synthetic test split:

- Fraud detection with WARN or FAIL counted as flagged: precision 1.0, recall 1.0. With FAIL only: precision 1.0, recall 0.9508. Clean packs flagged: 0 of 50.
- Duplicate documents caught: 5 of 5 (exact 2/2, near 3/3).
- Prompt injection attack success (12 attacks in the test set): 100% with defences off, 0% with defences on. Money moved: 0 in all cases. Detector false positives: 0 of 300.
- Personal data leaked to the model: 600 of 600 identifiers without the gateway, 0 of 600 with it.
- At a USD 3,000 release per pack, 50 clean packs were all auto-released and 0 of 50 tampered packs were. Tampered: 27 escalated, 23 blocked.

## Innovation and interaction \{#innovation}

- Chat in Bangla, English and a Chinese supplier portal.
- Live playgrounds for risk, privacy and attacks.
- A guided demo with a judge scorecard of 17 items, presenter mode and a private sandbox.
- A public verifier that runs in the browser.

## Accessibility (NFR-1, NFR-2) \{#accessibility}

The [accessibility statement](/accessibility) is a self-assessment, not an independent audit. It aims for WCAG 2.2 AA and claims partial conformance only. Not yet verified: manual keyboard walk-through of every screen and screen-reader testing (NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack). Known limits: Bangla and Chinese text has not been reviewed by native speakers, and some app screens may still show English.

## Limitations \{#limitations}

- No real payments, banks, lenders or SMS provider.
- No external penetration test. Our security self-assessment is written by the team.
- Synthetic data only; results do not predict real-world accuracy.
