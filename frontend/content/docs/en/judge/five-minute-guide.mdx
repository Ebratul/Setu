## What it is \{#what-it-is}

The `/judges` page ("Judge quick-start") is the fastest way into Setu. It offers a private sandbox, a list of things to try, a map from features to our requirements, links, QR codes and live system status. Everything is a simulated sandbox with fictional data. No model was trained.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="judge" /> The judge account is a read-only demo user.

## When to use it \{#when}

Use it first. It needs no account: the demo uses the judge profile `judge@demo.setu` and no password.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Create your sandbox (30 seconds)">
Open `/judges` and press "Create my sandbox". You get a private copy of the demo data. Nothing you do affects anyone else, and you can reset it.
<Screenshot name="judge/judges-page" route="/judges" as="judge" alt="The Judge quick-start page with the buttons Open the live demo and Create my sandbox." caption="The judge quick-start page." />
</Step>
<Step title="Watch the guided demo (2 minutes)">
Press Auto-play in the [guided demo](/docs/en/judge/guided-demo). If time is short, jump to step 9 (forged bill of lading) and step 10 (same invoice at a second lender).
</Step>
<Step title="Break something in the playgrounds (1.5 minutes)">
Move the amount in the [Risk Playground](/demo/risk-playground) and watch the tier change. Run the hidden-text attack in the [Attack Console](/demo/attack-console) with defences ON and OFF. See [Playgrounds](/docs/en/judge/playgrounds).
</Step>
<Step title="Verify the audit log (1 minute)">
Open [/verify](/verify), choose an exported log file and check it in your browser. See [Verify](/docs/en/judge/verify).
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the page shows \{#results}

| Block | What it is |
|---|---|
| Demo sign-in | The judge profile and how to sign in |
| What to try in 60 seconds | Five links: Guided demo, Risk Playground, Privacy Playground, Attack Console, Verify the audit log |
| Features mapped to the requirements | FR, SR and NFR items with a "Where to see it" link |
| Links and resources | Live site, documentation, source code, demo video, live status |
| QR codes | Open the live demo or this page from a phone |
| System status | Checked from your browser every 60 seconds |

Some resource links show "Link coming" until they are set before submission.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- Some links open signed-in screens. Press "Create my sandbox" first.
- The feature map follows our own requirements, not the official judging rubric. See [Criteria](/docs/en/judge/criteria).
- If the status panel says it cannot reach the agent API, tell the team.

## Related security notes \{#security}
