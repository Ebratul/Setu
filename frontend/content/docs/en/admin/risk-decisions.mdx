## What it is \{#what-it-is}

The **Risk engine** page lists every payment decision taken by rules, plus the thresholds and rules themselves. The decisions come from deterministic code. The AI does not decide them. All money is simulated.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="admin" /> Admins and judges read this page. It is view-only.

## When to use it \{#when}

- To see how many actions were auto-released, sent for approval or blocked.
- To find out why one action got a certain tier.
- To check the limits (for example the Tier 1 maximum amount).

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Risk engine">
In the sidebar choose **Risk engine**. Four counters show **Auto-released**, **One-tap approvals**, **OTP-required** and **Blocked**.
<Screenshot name="admin/risk-counters" route="/app/admin/risk" as="admin" alt="Four counter cards: Auto-released, One-tap approvals, OTP-required and Blocked." caption="Decision counters." />
</Step>
<Step title="Read Decisions per day">
The stacked chart shows decisions per day. Press **Show chart data as a table** for the numbers.
<Screenshot name="admin/risk-chart" route="/app/admin/risk" as="admin" alt="A stacked bar chart titled Decisions per day." caption="Decisions per day." />
</Step>
<Step title="Check Recent decisions">
Each row shows When, Deal, Action, Tier, Decision, Reasons and Origin. Press **Show rule trace** to see which rules fired.
<Screenshot name="admin/risk-recent" route="/app/admin/risk" as="admin" alt="The Recent decisions table with one row expanded to show its rule trace." caption="A decision with its rule trace." />
</Step>
<Step title="Read the Risk policy">
Lower on the page, **Risk policy** lists thresholds and rules in groups: Block, Tier 3 and Tier 2. Press **Show raw config/risk.yaml** to see the source file.
<Screenshot name="admin/risk-policy" route="/app/admin/risk" as="admin" alt="The Risk policy section with thresholds and rule groups." caption="The policy as loaded from the server." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Released automatically. |
| Tier 2 (One-tap approvals) | A person must tap to approve. <TierBadge tier={2} /> |
| Tier 3 (OTP-required) | OTP and a 10-minute cooling-off. <TierBadge tier={3} /> |
| Blocked | Nothing can proceed (for example a sanctions hit). |
| Allowed / Approval required / Blocked | The decision column. |
| Origin | Where the action started (for example a user screen or the agent). |

Thresholds shown: Tier 1 maximum amount, fraud score above (Tier 3), new beneficiary window in days, and cooling-off in minutes.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- The policy table is read-only. The policy is edited in the server file `backend/config/risk.yaml`, not in the web app.
- The highest tier wins, and a Block beats every tier.
- **Try the risk playground** opens `/demo/risk-playground`, a dry run that saves nothing.
