## What it is \{#what-it-is}

The admin console is the part of Setu that shows how the system protects money and data. It is a group of pages under the sidebar heading **Admin and security**. All money in Setu is simulated, and each page carries a "Simulated" badge.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="admin" /> Administrators see every page below. Judges see the same group except **Company review**, and they cannot resolve security events.

## When to use it \{#when}

- A new company asked to join and needs a KYB decision (KYB means "know your business": checking who a company is).
- You want proof that the audit log was not changed.
- You want to see why a payment was blocked or sent for approval.
- You want to check that no personal data reached the AI.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- Pages under `/demo` use the same data as the app but add playground tools. They are meant for showing, not for daily work.
- If a page says you have no access, your account is not an admin account.
- Tier 2 and Tier 3 payment approvals are not in this group. Buyers approve them: see [Approvals](/docs/en/buyer/approvals).

## Related security notes \{#security}

<Callout type="security" title="Admins cannot move money from here">
No admin page releases funds. Payments follow the permission tiers, and Tier 3 needs an OTP and a 10-minute cooling-off. See [Permission tiers](/docs/en/security/permission-tiers).
</Callout>
