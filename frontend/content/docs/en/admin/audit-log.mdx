## What it is \{#what-it-is}

Every action in Setu is written to a log. Each entry is signed with Ed25519 (a digital signature) and linked by a hash to the entry before it. A hash is a short fingerprint of data. Change one character and the chain breaks.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="admin" /> Admins see the full log with the Evidence section. <RoleBadge role="judge" /> Judges can read it too.

## When to use it \{#when}

- To prove that nothing was changed, removed or reordered.
- To see what the AI agent, a user or the system did, and at which tier.
- To show someone how tampering is detected.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Audit log">
In the sidebar choose **Audit log**. The page is titled **Signed audit log**. The **Evidence** section shows entries in the log, entries by tier and by action, and the last verification.
<Screenshot name="admin/audit-evidence" route="/app/admin/audit" as="admin" alt="The Evidence section with the entry count, bars by tier and by action, and the Verify now button." caption="The Evidence section on the admin audit page." />
</Step>
<Step title="Verify now">
Press **Verify now**. The page re-checks every hash and signature and shows a green "Verified" badge with the number of entries, or the first broken entry.
<Screenshot name="admin/audit-verify" route="/app/admin/audit" as="admin" alt="A green Verified badge next to the Verify now button." caption="Result of Verify now." />
</Step>
<Step title="Read the live chain">
Under **Live chain**, each block is one entry. The line between blocks is the hash link. New entries appear on the right. Press **Refresh now** to reload.
<Screenshot name="admin/audit-chain" route="/app/admin/audit" as="admin" alt="The Live chain row of linked blocks, each labelled with a sequence number and an action." caption="The Live chain." />
</Step>
<Step title="Open an entry">
Select a block. A side window shows the exact bytes that were hashed, the payload hash, the previous hash, the entry hash and the signature. Each field has a **Copy** button. Entries about releases also show a **Receipt** button.
<Screenshot name="admin/audit-entry" route="/app/admin/audit" as="admin" alt="The Audit entry drawer showing a Verified badge and the hash fields." caption="One audit entry in detail." />
</Step>
<Step title="Try Tamper and see">
In **Tamper and see**, choose an entry, choose a change (edit a value, delete the entry, or swap with the next entry) and press **Tamper with the copy**. The page works on a copy and shows where verification breaks. Press **Restore the live chain** to return.
<Screenshot name="admin/audit-tamper" route="/app/admin/audit" as="admin" alt="The Tamper and see panel showing a broken chain after an edit." caption="Tampering is detected in the copy." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified, N entries | All hashes, signatures and the order are correct. |
| Broken at seq N | Entry N is the first one not in its place. |
| entry contents no longer match the hash | Someone changed the entry. |
| signature does not match the key | The entry was not signed by Setu. |
| an entry is missing | A deleted entry left a gap in the numbers. |
| entries are out of order | The order was changed. |
| the link to the previous entry is broken | The entry was inserted or rewritten. |

The **Public key fingerprint** identifies the key used to check signatures. **Open the public verifier** goes to `/verify`, which checks an exported file in the browser.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- The Evidence section and the export link appear on `/app/admin/audit`. The page `/demo/audit` shows the chain and the tamper panel only.
- Tamper and see never changes the real log. A banner says "This is a copy".
- Verifying a log that was cut at the end can still succeed. Compare the head sequence number with a receipt or an earlier export.
- Reset of a demo sandbox adds a signed `DEMO_RESET` entry. It is not an error.
