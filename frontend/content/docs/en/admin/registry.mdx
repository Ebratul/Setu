## What it is \{#what-it-is}

The **Document hash registry** gives every invoice and evidence document a digital fingerprint (a hash) once. The same document cannot back two deals, or be pledged at two lenders. Setu stores only hashes and minimal metadata.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="admin" /> Admins and judges view the registry. It is read-only.

## When to use it \{#when}

- To see how many duplicate documents were caught.
- To check whether a document is active or already released.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Registry">
Choose **Registry** in the sidebar. The top card **Duplicates caught** splits the count into **Exact copies** and **Near copies**. A second card shows **Entries**, **Active** and **Released**.
<Screenshot name="admin/registry-counters" route="/app/admin/registry" as="admin" alt="The Duplicates caught card with exact and near counts, next to Entries, Active and Released." caption="Registry counters." />
</Step>
<Step title="Filter the table">
Under **Filters** choose a **Status** (All, Active, Released) and a **Duplicates caught** value (All, Exact, Near).
<Screenshot name="admin/registry-filters" route="/app/admin/registry" as="admin" alt="The filter group for status and duplicate result." caption="Filters." />
</Step>
<Step title="Read the entries">
**Registry entries** shows Hash, Document, Purpose, Lender, Deal, Registered, Seen and Hits (exact / near). Use **Previous** and **Next** to page.
<Screenshot name="admin/registry-table" route="/app/admin/registry" as="admin" alt="The Registry entries table with hash prefixes and status badges." caption="Registry entries." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Exact copy | The same file bytes (same sha256 hash). |
| Near copy | A re-saved or re-scanned copy with the same identity fields (seller, number, total, currency) for invoices. |
| Purpose | Escrow evidence for a deal, or financing collateral at a lender. |
| Active | Still blocks other uses. |
| Released | Kept for history; the deal ended and it no longer blocks. |
| Hits | How many exact and near attempts hit this entry. |

A duplicate fails the document check and raises a Tier 3 case. See [Permission tiers](/docs/en/security/permission-tiers).

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- Hashes show only a short prefix.
- A near copy needs the same document type. Photos and packing lists get exact matching only.
- The page has no buttons to edit or delete entries.
- The lender check ("Try to finance this invoice at Lender B") is on the deal detail page, not on this admin page.

## Related security notes \{#security}

<Callout type="security">
The registry answers "is this hash registered?" only to admin, judge and, for their own documents, buyers. Every hit is audited.
</Callout>
