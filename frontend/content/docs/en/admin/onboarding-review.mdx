## What it is \{#what-it-is}

New companies register themselves, then wait for review. KYB means "know your business": checking who a company is. The **Company onboarding review** page lists applications with an automatic result, so an admin can decide.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="admin" /> Only administrators see **Company review** in the sidebar. Other roles get a "no access" message.

## When to use it \{#when}

When a buyer or supplier has submitted a company application and cannot use Setu yet.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Company review">
The tabs are **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected** and **All**. Start on Pending.
<Screenshot name="admin/onboarding-queue" route="/app/admin/onboarding" as="admin" alt="The review queue with status tabs and a table of applications." caption="The review queue." />
</Step>
<Step title="Press Review on a row">
The table shows Company, Applicant, Automatic result, Status and Submitted. The detail panel lists company fields, the **Risk score**, the masked **Bank account** and each automatic check.
<Screenshot name="admin/onboarding-detail" route="/app/admin/onboarding" as="admin" alt="An application detail panel with the risk score and a list of checks." caption="Application detail." />
</Step>
<Step title="Approve">
Press **Approve**, then confirm. The company is activated and its bank account enabled.
<Screenshot name="admin/onboarding-approve" route="/app/admin/onboarding" as="admin" alt="The Approve this company dialog." caption="Approving." />
</Step>
<Step title="Or reject with a reason">
Press **Reject**. Write a **Reason** of at least 10 characters (up to 500). The applicant can read it. The company is suspended.
<Screenshot name="admin/onboarding-reject" route="/app/admin/onboarding" as="admin" alt="The Reject this company dialog with a Reason box." caption="Rejecting." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

| Automatic result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Clear | All checks passed. |
| Needs review | At least one check needs attention. |
| Blocked | The sanctions screening found a hit. The score is forced to 0. |

Checks use pass, needs attention or failed. They include tax ID format, account holder matching the company name, licence name, country and sanctions screening.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- By default every application waits for an admin. Automatic approval of clear applications is a server setting that is off unless turned on.
- A decision is final. It cannot be undone.
- You cannot decide your own application.
- If someone else already decided, the list refreshes and shows a message.
