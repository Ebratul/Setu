## What it is \{#what-it-is}

The **Privacy Gateway** page proves that every message to the AI is masked first. A mask replaces a real value, such as a bank account number, with a token like ACCT_1. Code puts the real value back after the answer returns.

## Who uses it \{#who}

<RoleBadge role="admin" /> Admins and judges.

## When to use it \{#when}

- To confirm that **Leaked** stays at 0.
- To see exactly what the AI received.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Privacy">
Choose **Privacy** in the sidebar. Three numbers appear: **Identifiers protected**, **Leaked** ("Should always stay at 0") and **AI calls**.
<Screenshot name="admin/privacy-totals" route="/app/admin/privacy" as="admin" alt="Three tiles: Identifiers protected, Leaked and AI calls." caption="Privacy totals." />
</Step>
<Step title="Read Protected by type and the daily chart">
**Protected by type** counts masked items by kind. The chart shows identifiers protected per day for the last 14 days. Use **Show as table** for numbers.
<Screenshot name="admin/privacy-chart" route="/app/admin/privacy" as="admin" alt="A chart of identifiers protected per day over 14 days." caption="Identifiers protected per day." />
</Step>
<Step title="Inspect Latest transparency records">
Each record shows its step and source. Press **Show masked payload** to read what was prepared. A label says **Sent to the AI** or **Prepared, not sent**. Only masked values are shown.
<Screenshot name="admin/privacy-records" route="/app/admin/privacy" as="admin" alt="A transparency record expanded to show a masked payload with tokens." caption="A masked payload." />
</Step>
<Step title="Prove it">
In **Prove it**, **Open the LangSmith run** shows the trace. If tracing is off, the page says so.
<Screenshot name="admin/privacy-prove" route="/app/admin/privacy" as="admin" alt="The Prove it card with a link to the LangSmith run." caption="The Prove it card." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean \{#results}

- **Leaked** counts values that slipped past masking in an outgoing message. They are masked again, but the count should be 0.
- The page also states if **Masking is on** and if **Amounts are masked**.
- For the privacy measurement on the test split, see [Evaluation](/docs/en/admin/evaluation).

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- Company names, addresses and goods descriptions are not treated as personal identifiers.
- A token from an earlier chat turn cannot be restored later. The answer shows a protected label instead.
- **Open the privacy playground** (`/demo/privacy-playground`) lets you test text without calling the AI.

## Related security notes \{#security}

<Callout type="security">
Every AI call goes through one client that masks first. Real values stay in memory and are never stored in the privacy records.
</Callout>
