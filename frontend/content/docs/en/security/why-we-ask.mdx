## What it is \{#what-it-is}

Setu helps importers pay Chinese suppliers through milestone escrow. Even in a sandbox, we treat the process like a real one: we need to know that a company is real before it can move money, even simulated money.

## Why we ask \{#why}

| We ask for | So that |
|---|---|
| Name, job title, verified mobile | A real person is accountable for the account and can approve sensitive actions. |
| Licence, IRC, TIN or USCC | A reviewer can confirm the company exists and is not registered twice. |
| Bank details (suppliers) | Payouts go to an account in the company's own name. |
| Documents | A reviewer can compare them with what you typed. |
| Consent | You agree to the checks, the Terms and the Privacy notice. |

## How it is protected \{#protection}

- Sensitive numbers are encrypted when stored and shown masked, for example `****1234`.
- A reviewer must write a reason of at least 10 characters to see a full value. The reason and the field name are written to the audit log, never the value itself.
- Before any text reaches an AI model, personal and business identifiers are masked. Numbers come from code, not from the model.
- Instructions found inside uploaded documents are treated as data, never as commands.

## Your choices \{#choices}

In **Account settings** you can download your data and request deletion. You can read the [Terms](/terms) and the [Privacy notice](/privacy) at any time. Both are marked as sandbox pages and show their version.
