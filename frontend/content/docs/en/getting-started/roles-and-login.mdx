## What it is \{#what-it-is}

Setu has four roles. What you can see and do depends on your role. There are two ways to get in: the **demo login** (pick a ready-made account, no password) and **real sign-in** (email and password).

## Who uses it \{#who}

| Role | What the role is for |
|---|---|
| <RoleBadge role="buyer" /> | An importer. Orders, cash flow, financing, escrow, approvals, chat. Lands on `/app`. |
| <RoleBadge role="supplier" /> | A Chinese supplier. Confirms milestone schedules, uploads documents. Lands on `/supplier`. |
| <RoleBadge role="admin" /> | Reviews new companies, incidents, the registry and the audit log. |
| <RoleBadge role="judge" /> | A read-only demo user with a view across the demo data. It cannot confirm milestone schedules. |

## Demo login or real sign-in \{#modes}

The `/login` page checks which mode the deployment uses.

- **Demo mode.** The page title is "Log in as a demo user". It lists pre-made sandbox accounts, each with a "Log in" button. No password. A note says this screen "does not exist in a real deployment".
- **Real sign-in.** The title is "Sign in". Enter your email and password.

The pages `/register`, `/forgot-password`, `/reset-password`, `/verify-email`, `/onboarding`, `/onboarding/status` and `/account-status` only work when real sign-in is enabled. In demo mode they show a short note with a link to the demo login.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the login page">
Go to `/login`. In demo mode choose an account and press "Log in". Buyers go to `/app`, suppliers to `/supplier`.

## Company onboarding and KYB status \{#onboarding}

KYB means "know your business": checking that a company is real. A new sign-in cannot use trade features until a person at Setu approves the company.

1. On `/onboarding` fill four steps: account type and your name (Buyer or Supplier), company, bank account, review and submit.
2. Press "Submit for review". Setu runs automatic checks and shows them on `/onboarding/status`.
3. The status page refreshes about every 20 seconds. When it says "Approved", Setu opens your session.

<Screenshot name="getting-started/onboarding-status" route="/onboarding/status" as="buyer" alt="The application status page showing Waiting for review and a list of automatic checks with passed or needs attention labels." caption="Review status and automatic checks." />

The checks include tax ID format, whether the account holder matches the company name, bank account type, licence name, country and bank country. The holder name must match the company legal name, otherwise the application gets a closer review. A sanctions check also runs but is hidden from the applicant's list.

`/account-status` explains why you cannot enter yet: no Setu account, awaiting verification, or suspended. A rejected application shows the reviewer's reason.

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- "Too many attempts": wait a few minutes.
- "Your email is not verified yet": finish the 6-digit code step first.
- Admin and Judge cannot be chosen at onboarding. Those accounts are not self-service.

## Related security notes \{#security}

<Callout type="security">
Demo accounts exist only in a sandbox. A judge account is read-only, so it cannot approve or move simulated money.
</Callout>
