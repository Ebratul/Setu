## What it is \{#what-it-is}

Setu is a competition prototype. Every money movement is a **simulated sandbox** entry. You see the label "Simulated" next to money, the banner "Simulated sandbox – fictional data" in the demo, and a footer line "Simulated sandbox — no real payments". No model was trained: Setu calls hosted language models, and only to explain results.

<Callout type="warning" title="No real money">
There are no real payment rails, no real bank, no real lender and no real SMS provider. Do not enter real bank details or real personal data.
</Callout>

## What is simulated \{#simulated}

| Part | What this means |
|---|---|
| Money | Escrow deposits, releases, FX conversion and loans are entries in a simulated double-entry ledger. A ledger records each amount as a matching debit and credit. |
| People and companies | Rahima Knitwear, the Chinese suppliers and the lenders are fictional. |
| Lenders | Lender A, Lender B, supplier credit and the escrow-backed loan are mock lenders. |
| SMS and OTP | One-time passwords arrive in an on-screen "Demo phone", not a real phone. |
| Time skips | The demo can credit simulated customer payments in step 12 so the last tranche can be funded. This is a labelled and audited time skip. |
| AI answers in the demo | By default a sandbox replays recorded answers, so every run is the same and works offline. "Use live AI" switches to a live model when the server has a key, up to a limited budget. |

## What is real \{#real}

The software that makes the decisions is real and runs on every action.

- **Calculations.** Landed cost, margin, cash gap, financing ranking and FX are computed by Python code with exact decimals.
- **Risk tiers.** The tier of every action is decided by code, not by the AI.
- **Document forensics.** PDFs are really parsed, hidden text is really found, hashes are really compared.
- **Privacy Gateway.** Identifiers are really masked before any model call.
- **Audit log.** Entries are really signed (Ed25519) and hash-chained. You can verify an export yourself on [/verify](/docs/en/judge/verify).

## Your own sandbox \{#own}

In the demo each judge gets a private copy of the data. Nothing you do affects anyone else, you can reset it, and it expires after some hours.
