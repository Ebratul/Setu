## What it is \{#what-it-is}

A fast walk through Setu: the public landing page first, then the screens a buyer uses on one order, then the supplier and admin views. Each stop links to the real screen. Money is simulated.

## Step by step \{#how-to}

<Steps>
<Step title="Landing page (about 20 seconds)">
The home page has the headline, a "Run the guided demo" button, "Start my own sandbox" and "Judge mode". Sections follow: how it works, features, security, demo and evaluation results.
<Screenshot name="getting-started/landing" route="/" as="judge" alt="The Setu landing page with the hero headline and the buttons Run the guided demo, Start my own sandbox and Judge mode." caption="The public landing page." />
</Step>
<Step title="Chat (/app/chat)">
Ask about an order in Bangla or English. The answer shows cards built from numbers that code calculated.
<Screenshot name="getting-started/chat" route="/app/chat" as="buyer" alt="The chat screen with a question about landed cost and an answer with result cards." caption="Explained chat." />
</Step>
<Step title="Analysis, cash flow and financing">
[Analysis](/app/analysis) shows landed cost and margin. [Cash flow](/app/cashflow) shows a 90-day calendar with alerts. [Financing](/app/financing) compares options on total cost.
<Screenshot name="getting-started/cashflow" route="/app/cashflow" as="buyer" alt="The cash-flow page with a 90-day calendar and a shortfall alert." caption="Cash flow with a shortfall alert." />
</Step>
<Step title="Suppliers and deals">
[Suppliers](/app/suppliers) shows KYB checks. [Deals](/app/deals) shows the milestone schedule and the escrow card, where tranches are released.
<Screenshot name="getting-started/deals" route="/app/deals" as="buyer" alt="The deals list with simulated escrow status for each deal." caption="Deals and escrow." />
</Step>
<Step title="Documents and email check">
[Documents](/app/deals) are checked for mismatches and hidden text. [Email check](/app/inbox/email-check) flags a payment-redirection email.
</Step>
<Step title="Approvals">
[Approvals](/app/approvals) lists requests that need a person: one tap for Tier 2, OTP and cooling-off for Tier 3.
<Screenshot name="getting-started/approvals" route="/app/approvals" as="buyer" alt="The approvals page listing pending requests with their permission tier." caption="Approvals by tier." />
</Step>
<Step title="Supplier portal (/supplier)">
A supplier confirms the milestone schedule and sees the deal. The interface can be in Chinese.
</Step>
<Step title="Admin and audit">
Admins use the registry, risk, privacy and audit screens under `/app/admin`. Anyone can check an exported log at [/verify](/docs/en/judge/verify).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Tips and common mistakes \{#tips}

- The fastest route is the guided demo: 12 narrated steps.
- Links to `/app` need a signed-in session. In demo mode use the login page first.

## Related security notes \{#security}

<Callout type="security">
Tier 0 and 1 are automatic, Tier 2 is one-tap approval, Tier 3 needs OTP plus a 10-minute cooling-off. The tier is decided by code.
</Callout>
